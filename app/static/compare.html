02 / DATA INTERPRETATION

What these comparisons show

Market capitalization

Provider-reported figures, which may be unknown for some projects. No market-cap number is calculated from unverified supply.

Volume and movement

24-hour observed trading volume and price change may differ by market, exchange coverage and provider timestamps.

Identity

Each row is keyed by its full provider ID. Similar ticker symbols do not mean two assets represent the same network or contract.
